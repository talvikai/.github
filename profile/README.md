# Talvik

Building open infrastructure for reusable AI artifacts, knowledge continuity, and interoperability.

## Our Mission

Talvik develops open protocols and developer tools that help AI systems reuse knowledge, preserve context, and collaborate across platforms.

## Featured Project

### MAK4I

**Memory, Artifacts & Knowledge for Intelligence**

> The protocol for not rebuilding what your AI already built.

MAK4I is an open protocol focused on:

- Reusable AI artifacts
- Knowledge continuity
- Check-before-create workflows
- AI interoperability
- Open standards
